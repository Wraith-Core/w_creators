# NUI contract

The binding NUI contract is **[ARCHITECTURE.md §8](ARCHITECTURE.md#8-nui-contract)**: the Lua → NUI
messages (`editor`, `editorClose`, `panel`, `panelClose`, `charCard`), the node shape (§8.2), the
NUI → Lua callbacks (`editor:action`, `editor:camera`, `panel:action`, `panel:close`, §8.3), the
control ids (§8.4), dirty/loading behaviour (§8.5) and the camera presets (§8.6).

In short: Lua (`client/editor/`) builds a translated tree of semantic nodes; the React NUI
(`web/src/`) renders it and sends back control ids and values. The NUI never sees a component hash,
a native, a price formula or untranslated copy (the one fixed text is the brand wordmark, §0.7). The pre-v3 flat payload (`categories`, `features`,
`overlays`, `action:'component'`, `part:'pant'`…) is retired.

Tests: `web/tests/*.test.mjs` render every editor mode, the panel and the character card from the
payload fixtures (`web/tests/fixtures/payloads.json`, generated by `generate_payloads.py`).

## Result extension 1.0.4

Failed paid operations may return `REFUND_PENDING` or `REFUND_REVIEW` with `data.refundId`,
`data.amount`, `data.currency` and `data.refunded`. A known retained charge stays in `charged`;
an uncertain debit is marked `data.paymentUncertain`. The editor appends the translated receipt ID
to its existing error message. The NUI still receives one translated `message` and needs no new action.

Refund receipts also retain the account owner and immutable character generation (`players.id`).
Wraith Core's `WithCitizenGeneration` lease surrounds the paid operation and later compensation;
reassignment of the citizen number never transfers an older generation's refund to its new holder.
Other frameworks do not automatically retry failed compensation without that generation lease.

A boolean money-bridge refusal after an attempted debit/credit is treated as uncertain and sent to review; only a compensation not yet attempted can resume automatically.
